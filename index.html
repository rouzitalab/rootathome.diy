About

One install per home.
Every device in the family.

root@home is a household media server you run yourself — upload, browse, thumbnails, and playback on the devices you already own. No cloud lease. No app store tax. Just your LAN and your files.

01

Own both ends

You own the clients and the storage. We’re building so you can own the pipe too.

02

Home Wi‑Fi first

v0 is LAN-native — phone and laptop browsers talking to a box in your house.

03

Shared, not leaked

Albums and per-file access for the household — admin without private-file bypass.
