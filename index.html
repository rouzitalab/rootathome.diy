The idea

Bring it home.

root@home runs quietly on a Mac, Windows PC or Linux machine that stays on in your house. Every phone, laptop and TV at home connects straight to it, so your photos travel across your own Wi‑Fi instead of across the world.

During the beta, the app and server send small anonymous health pings, such as app version, crash counts and free disk space, so we can fix problems early. They never include filenames, photos, messages or your name, and they stop when root@home leaves beta.
